How I work

Build, Measure, Repeat

I like picking projects that are new to me and a little too ambitious: computer vision, local AI, robots, and hardware I haven't used before. That's how I learn the fastest.

I also test my work honestly. I built a forex signal scanner, then backtested it and found it had no real edge (about 46% when it needed 55.6%). So I turned it into a research and journal tool instead. Measure before you believe.
