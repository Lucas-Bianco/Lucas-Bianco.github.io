2026 · Python + computer vision

Pokémon Card Recognition Platform

You take a photo of a Pokémon card and the app tells you which card it is and what it's worth. It matches the photo against a catalog of 20,444 cards using CLIP image embeddings, a FAISS search index, and OCR to read the card text.

The hard part was finding the card in a messy phone photo. I tested 99 real phone scans and scored each detection strategy by whether recognition got the right card. That raised detection coverage from 31% to 63% with zero regressions. It taught me to measure every change instead of guessing.

Built with

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • React PWA
  • CLIP
  • FAISS
  • OCR

2026 · Raspberry Pi 5

Jarvis Home Hub

Jarvis is my own home hub: a wall kiosk, a touchscreen, and a phone app that show my goals, daily plan, calendar, and a radar of my projects. It runs on Raspberry Pi 5 boards and talks to my phone over Tailscale.

I made it local-first, so the AI runs on local Ollama models and it costs nothing to run. The first stages (Stage 0 and 1A) are deployed and I use them every day. Building it in small stages kept it working the whole time.

2026 · Electron + Node

ForexAI Research

I built a desktop app that scanned currency charts for short-term trading signals. Then I backtested it honestly on data it had never seen.

The result: it had no real edge. It called short-term direction right about 46% of the time, and it needed 55.6% just to break even. So I turned it into a research and journal tool instead. The lesson I took away: measure before you believe.

Built with

  • Electron
  • Node.js
  • JavaScript

Out-of-sample: ~46% correct vs 55.6% needed
